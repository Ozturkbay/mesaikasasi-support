Support

Common questions about Mesai Kasası. The app’s interface is in Turkish; Turkish names are shown in italics. Can’t find an answer? Email feesheet.support@icloud.com.

Getting started

On first launch, enter your monthly salary as gross (Brüt) or net. Entering the gross from your payslip gives the most accurate result. Monthly hours (225), multipliers and your weekly rest day are under Ayarlar › Maaş ve çarpanlar.

Logging a day

Tap a day on the calendar. The app suggests a kind: holiday work on public holidays, rest day work on your rest day, overtime otherwise. Type the hours or tap a quick-hours chip, then Kaydet. Annual leave (Yıllık izin) and sick report (Rapor) need no hours.

How estimates are calculated

  • Hourly gross wage = monthly gross ÷ monthly hours (225 by default).
  • Overtime 1.5× (Labour Law art. 41); weekly rest day work 1.5×.
  • Public holiday work 2× (art. 47). Your salary already pays for the holiday, so the extra is one day’s wage: hours × hourly × (2 − 1). Adjustable.
  • Estimated net (Full Version): gross minus SGK employee share (14%) and unemployment insurance (1%) up to the SGK ceiling, income tax on the cumulative annual base, and stamp tax (0.759%), with the minimum wage income and stamp tax exemptions applied.
  • 2026 values: minimum wage 33,030.00 gross / 28,075.50 net; SGK ceiling 297,270.00; wage income tax brackets 15% to 190,000, 20% to 400,000, 27% to 1,500,000, 35% to 5,300,000, 40% above. Sources: Turkish Revenue Administration (GİB), SGK circular 2026/2, Official Gazette no. 33119.

Why does it differ from my payslip?

All results are estimates, not payroll, legal or tax advice. Common reasons: a raise during the year (earlier months use today’s salary), starting mid-year, allowances and deductions the app does not model (meal, transport, bonuses, pension, union dues, advances, disability allowance), sick or unpaid days, a contract with different multipliers or monthly hours, and employer rounding.

Public holidays

The app includes Türkiye’s 2026 and 2027 official holidays, including half-day eves from 13:00 (Ramadan Feast 20–22 March 2026 and 9–11 March 2027; Feast of Sacrifice 27–30 May 2026 and 16–19 May 2027). Administrative leave days for public servants are not holidays and are not marked.

Full Version and restoring it

The free version shows an occasional ad after you save a day or open a month’s summary — never while you type. You can open the net estimate, history or export for 24 hours with the Reklam izle, 24 saat aç button on a locked card. The Full Version (Tam Sürüm) removes all ads and permanently unlocks the net estimate and payslip breakdown, history, PDF/CSV export, the widget and the reminder. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription. To restore it on a new iPhone, tap Ayarlar › Satın alımı geri yükle while signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Widget, Siri and reminder

Add the widget from the Home Screen editor (Full Version). Say “Mesai Kasası ile mesai ekle” to Siri or use the Mesai ekle action in Shortcuts or on the Action button. Set the reminder under Ayarlar › Mesai hatırlatıcısı.

Language

The app is in Turkish and English. From version 1.0.1, choose the language in Settings › Dil / Language: System follows your iPhone’s language; Türkçe or English switches the app right away, without a restart, and the widget uses the same language. The app name, permission prompts and Siri phrases follow your iPhone’s language. Amounts are in Turkish lira in both languages.

Your data

Your log stays on your iPhone; the app does not sync. Restoring a device backup to a new phone brings your log with it. You can also export each month as CSV.
