Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iPhone app Mesai Kasası: Fazla Mesai (“Mesai Kasası” or “the app”) handles information. The app is developed and published by independent developer Fatih Öztürkbay (“we”).

Summary

  • Nothing you enter reaches us. Your overtime log, salary and settings are stored only on your device and are never shared with the ad provider.
  • There is no account, analytics or server that receives your data.
  • The free version shows occasional Google AdMob ads when you finish or start a task. Google’s ad software collects data such as a device identifier, approximate location (from the IP address) and ad interactions (section 3).
  • Personalized ads need your permission: Google’s consent message first (EEA/UK), then Apple’s tracking prompt. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • The Full Version (one-time purchase) removes all ads; no ads are loaded once it is active. Apple handles the payment; your payment details never reach us.

1. Information we collect

None. We (the developer) do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports, and none of it is sent to us. The data Google collects to show ads in the free version is described in section 3.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Day entries: date, kind (overtime, rest day work, holiday work, annual leave, sick report), hours and an optional note.
  • Your pay settings: gross or net monthly salary, monthly hours, multipliers and weekly rest day.
  • Reminder settings and whether the Full Version is unlocked.
  • A small summary of this month (hours and estimated amount) for the Home Screen widget, kept in an app group shared only with the app’s own widget.

Salary information can be sensitive, so it is never sent anywhere; it is used on your device only to calculate estimates.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads with Google LLC’s Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads appear only after you save a day entry or when you open a month’s summary — never while you enter data, during first use, on the purchase screen or in Settings. You can also choose to watch a short “rewarded” ad to open a locked feature for 24 hours.

To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google’s ad software may collect:

  • Device identifier: Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking; otherwise app- or developer-scoped identifiers.
  • Approximate location: general location estimated from the IP address (no precise GPS location).
  • Advertising data and interactions: which ads were shown, taps, video views and app launches.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.

This data goes to Google and its advertising partners, not to us. Your overtime log, salary, notes and estimates are never given to the ad software. Ad content is capped at the “PG” rating and the app is not marked as child-directed. Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

There is no sign-in and no analytics. Tax and social security parameters and the holiday calendar ship inside the app.

4. Consent and tracking permission

Before any ad loads, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for the consent required where you live (for example, a consent message in the EEA and UK). iOS then asks whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). The ad software starts only after these steps.

If you decline, the app works the same; only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and, where offered, in the app under Ayarlar › Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri.

5. Full Version and purchases

The Full Version (“Tam Sürüm”) is a one-time in-app purchase through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your card details or Apple Account. The app checks the purchase on device with Apple’s StoreKit. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles this data. While the Full Version is active, no ads are loaded or shown.

6. Notifications

The optional overtime reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. No push notifications are used and nothing is sent for them.

7. Siri, Shortcuts and widget

The “Mesai ekle” shortcut runs on your device and writes to the app’s own storage. If you use it with Siri, Apple’s Siri privacy terms apply to your voice requests; that information does not reach us. Shortcuts and the widget never show ads.

8. Export

PDF and CSV files are created on your device and go only where you send them from the share sheet.

9. Device backups and new phones

Apple may include app data in your iCloud or computer backup. Those backups belong to your Apple Account; we cannot access them. The app does not sync to any cloud on its own.

10. Deleting your data

Delete all day entries in the app under Ayarlar › Tüm kayıtları sil, or delete the app to remove all of its data. For data Google collects for advertising, use Google’s My Ad Center and iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

11. Children

Mesai Kasası is intended for working adults and is not directed at children. We collect no personal data from anyone; ad content is capped at the “PG” rating.

12. Changes

If the app ever changes how it handles data (for example, by adding cloud sync), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
