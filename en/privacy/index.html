Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iPhone app Mesai Kasası: Fazla Mesai (“Mesai Kasası” or “the app”) handles information. The app is developed and published by independent developer Fatih Öztürkbay (“we”).

Summary

  • Mesai Kasası collects no data. Nothing you enter reaches us.
  • Your overtime log, salary and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or server.
  • The Full Version purchase is handled by Apple; your payment details never reach us.
  • The App Store privacy label states “Data Not Collected”.

1. Information we collect

None. Mesai Kasası does not collect, store or transmit your name, email, location, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Day entries: date, kind (overtime, rest day work, holiday work, annual leave, sick report), hours and an optional note.
  • Your pay settings: gross or net monthly salary, monthly hours, multipliers and weekly rest day.
  • Reminder settings and whether the Full Version is unlocked.
  • A small summary of this month (hours and estimated amount) for the Home Screen widget, kept in an app group shared only with the app’s own widget.

Salary information can be sensitive, so it is never sent anywhere; it is used on your device only to calculate estimates.

3. No account, server or analytics

There is no sign-in. Apart from App Store purchases, the app does not connect to the internet. It contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking. Tax and social security parameters and the holiday calendar ship inside the app.

4. Full Version and purchases

The Full Version (“Tam Sürüm”) is a one-time in-app purchase through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your card details or Apple Account. The app checks the purchase on device with Apple’s StoreKit. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles this data.

5. Notifications

The optional overtime reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. No push notifications are used and nothing is sent for them.

6. Siri, Shortcuts and widget

The “Mesai ekle” shortcut runs on your device and writes to the app’s own storage. If you use it with Siri, Apple’s Siri privacy terms apply to your voice requests; that information does not reach us.

7. Export

PDF and CSV files are created on your device and go only where you send them from the share sheet.

8. Device backups and new phones

Apple may include app data in your iCloud or computer backup. Those backups belong to your Apple Account; we cannot access them. The app does not sync to any cloud on its own.

9. Deleting your data

Delete all day entries in the app under Ayarlar › Tüm kayıtları sil, or delete the app to remove all of its data.

10. Children

Mesai Kasası is intended for working adults. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

11. Changes

If the app ever changes how it handles data (for example, by adding cloud sync), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
